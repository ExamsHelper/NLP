PROGRAM: 4.a) synonyms
======================

CODE:
'''4.a) synonyms'''

import nltk
nltk.download('wordnet')
from nltk.corpus import wordnet

words = set()
for syn in wordnet.synsets("good"):
    for lemma in syn.lemmas():
        words.add(lemma.name())

print(words)

PROGRAM: 4.b) antonyms
======================

CODE:
'''4.b) antonyms'''

import nltk
nltk.download('wordnet')
from nltk.corpus import wordnet

words = set()
for syn in wordnet.synsets("good"):
    for lemma in syn.lemmas():
        for ant in lemma.antonyms():
            words.add(ant.name())

print(words)

OUTPUT:
{'badness', 'ill', 'evilness', 'evil', 'bad'}
